Me, Myself, and I

Hi! My name is Lisa. I am a student at Northern Virginia Community College. I am currently studying Contract Management and hope to continue my education at George Mason University.

One of the things I enjoy is collecting cute figures. Pop Mart has many different characters and collectibles. I like how each collection has its own style and personality, I am mainly a fan of Hirono's since each series comes with a unique story, each representing a different struggle in life giving us a chance to feel seen in a way.

One of my favorite artists is Claude Monet. I really enjoy his paintings because of his use of color, light, and nature. I also find art museums interesting because they allow people to explore different artists and styles.
